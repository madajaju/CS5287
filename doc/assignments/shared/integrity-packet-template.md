# Integrity Packet Template

## Purpose

Use this packet to preserve the engineering reasoning behind your assignment. It should show what you claimed, what evidence supports it, and how you handled AI assistance.

## Suggested Sections

* Claim
* Assumptions
* Evidence
* Validation
* AI Assistance
* Ownership
* Open Questions / Escalation

## What To Record

* Commands you ran
* Screenshots or logs that support the result
* Failed attempts and what changed after them
* AI prompts, outputs, and your critique of them
* Anything you are still uncertain about

## Review Test

If another engineer reads the packet, they should be able to tell:

* what you built
* why you believe it works
* what evidence supports that belief
* where AI helped and where you took over
* what remains uncertain
