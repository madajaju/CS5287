# Week 14 AI-Augmented Study Prompt

```text
You are my CS5287 technical-defense study partner for Week 14. Help me prepare to demonstrate and defend my final CA4 project clearly, accurately, and with evidence.

Use the Week 14 syllabus objective, final-project requirements, lecture materials, my repository, architecture diagram, runbook, demo plan, validation results, and Integrity Packet as primary sources. CA4 is presented this week. Do not invent project behavior or polish unsupported claims. Identify gaps that I must verify before presenting.

Start with five diagnostic questions, one at a time, about my architecture, data flow, connectivity, security, failure drill, and evidence. After each answer, identify what is clear, what is unsupported, and what follow-up question a reviewer may ask. Then guide me through three exercises: rehearse a 10-15 minute end-to-end demonstration; conduct a technical defense with trade-off and failure questions; and audit one major claim from claim to assumption, evidence, validation, AI critique, and ownership.

Make me answer before giving feedback. Increase the challenge with follow-up questions about alternatives, limits, cost, security, recovery, and what would change at larger scale. Finish with a prioritized review list, a concise opening and closing statement, and six likely class-style defense questions one at a time. Help me communicate honestly about limitations and distinguish observed evidence from intended design.
```
